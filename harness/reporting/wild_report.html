Frozen conditions

How scoring works

  • 36 fixed tasks, six per domain.
  • Three independent attempts per agent/task.
  • Task score = arithmetic mean of its three attempts.
  • Domain score = mean of its six task scores.
  • Overall score = mean of the six domain scores.
  • Native partial scores and genuine deadline outcomes retained.
  • Superseded and withdrawn attempts preserved outside ranking.

Concurrency and cleanup

  • Up to nine independent attempts for one task cohort.
  • Fresh mutable workspace and profile for each attempt.
  • Shared immutable images/dependencies reused across the cohort.
  • Native deadlines; no arbitrary 30-minute cutoff.
  • Export and grade before removing containers.
  • Task-owned resources retired after their final consumer.
  • No global Docker prune; large records retained in place.
